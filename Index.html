💌 Happy 16th Birthday, Shubham! 🎂🤍

Hey, birthday boy! 😂✨ So, you're sixteen now! Does that mean you'll stop overthinking and finally realise how capable you are? Probably not—but I'm still going to remind you! 🤭

I never expected that meeting you would become one of the things I'd be grateful for about my Class 10 journey, but here we are. I'm genuinely glad our paths crossed. Thank you for your kind heart, the respect you show me, and the little things you do that mean more than you realise. You make me feel valued and cared for, and that's something I truly appreciate. 🫶🏻

Since this is our last year of school, I hope we make more memories worth smiling about. We don't know where life will take us after Class 10 or how things might change, but I'm grateful you're part of this chapter of my life. I hope that, years from now, we'll be able to look back on these days and smile at the memories we've made. 🌷

And Shubham, please believe in yourself a little more. One bad day doesn't define you, and one mistake doesn't erase your abilities. Don't let your overthinking convince you that you're not good enough. Be patient with yourself, keep working towards your dreams, and remember to give yourself the same kindness you give others. You don't have to have everything figured out at sixteen. You're still growing, learning, and discovering what you're capable of. ❤️

I hope this new year of your life brings you happiness, new opportunities, unforgettable memories, and plenty of reasons to be proud of yourself. I hope you find the courage to face difficult days, the confidence to trust your abilities, and the peace to appreciate how far you've come. Keep that kind heart, keep that smile, and please don't let overthinking steal too many of your happy moments! 😂🤍

And if you ever read this letter again on a day when things don't go your way, remember this: you don't have to be perfect to be someone worth believing in. Keep trying, keep growing, and never underestimate what you can achieve when you give yourself a fair chance.

Happy Birthday to someone who became a beautiful, unexpected part of my school story. I'm glad I met you, and I genuinely wish to see you grow into someone who believes in himself as much as the people who believe in him. ✨

Here's to sixteen, the memories we've made, the moments still waiting to happen, and all the wonderful chapters life has ahead for you.

Happy Birthday once again, Shubham! 🎂💌
